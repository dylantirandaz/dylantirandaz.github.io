Emergent topography: what language models think Earth looks like, in 3D

Epistemic status: rough draft. Land/water replications are scored against ground truth (n=4050/grid); elevation claims rest on threshold fields plus landmark spot checks, with no gridded elevation ground truth. Single seed throughout; no error bars. Frontier runs cost ~$200; local runs $0.

0. Summary

Per-coordinate binary probes reproduce Henry's blind-map result locally, then extend it along a third axis: elevation. At 7–9B parameters, models recover coarse continents but no relief; at frontier scale they recover both, to 99% coastline accuracy with correctly placed cordilleras, plateaus, and abyssal plains. A battery of landmark probes indicates the operative variable is coordinate-to-place indexing precision, which is coarse (~103 km errors) in small models and precise at frontier. All claims below are about retrieval under independent local queries; nothing here required the model to see a map, neighboring cells, or its own prior answers.

1. Background

Henry (2025) queried language models about individual lat/lon coordinates (“Land or Water?”), took first-token logprobs, and assembled the resulting probability field into equirectangular maps. Small models produced degenerate priors (“all land”); geographic structure emerged with scale. The present work replicates that procedure on local hardware, then adds elevation: binary above/below-threshold probes at +3000m, +1000m, −1000m, and −4000m, assembled into hypsometric bands and rendered as relief globes.

2. Method

2.1 Grid

Equirectangular cell centers at 4° (90×45 = 4050 cells); pilot at 8° (990 cells; note 8 ∤ 180, so coverage stops at 82°N). Each cell is one independent API call with zero shared context. Query order is shuffled per run (seed 3).

2.2 Prompts (exact)

Land/water:
Location: 45.0° N, 90.0° W
If this location is over land, say 'Land'. If this location is over water, say 'Water'. Reply with only the word.

Threshold T (example T=+1000):
Location: 45.0° N, 90.0° W
If this location is above 1000 meters elevation, say 'Yes'. Otherwise say 'No'. Reply with only the word.

Negative thresholds substitute “below N meters elevation (i.e. deeper ocean than Nm)”. Coordinates lead the prompt. This ordering is load-bearing: instruction-first ordering combined with the Ollama chat template collapses Qwen2.5-7B to 96.5% Land (accuracy 0.371); coordinates-first recovers oceans at confident probabilities. Raw (template-free) completions behave like the coordinates-first variant, implicating prefix/template interaction rather than the wording itself.

2.3 Readout

Temperature 0 throughout. Local models: first-token logprobs (top 15), softmaxed over the two target labels with prefix fallback for split tokens. Frontier models (no logprob API): hard 0/1 from parsed text, with unparseable responses recorded as missing rather than imputed. Frontier reasoning was set to each model's measured floor (Astra: minimal, ~12 thinking tokens; Opus: minimal, ~95; Fable: low, ~0) after confirming that unconstrained reasoning multiplies cost without changing top-1 answers on probe cells.

2.4 Models

Local (Ollama 4-bit quants): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Llama3.1-8B, Gemma2-9B. Frontier via OpenRouter: GPT-6-Astra, Claude-Fable-5.1, Claude-Opus-5.5. Qwen3-8B is reported only where it produced parseable output; its OpenAI-compatible endpoint returns no usable logprobs (thinking-block interaction), and native-API runs were land-biased mush. It is retained as a documented negative.

2.5 Ground truth and metrics

Natural Earth 110m land polygons, rasterized to the query grid. Land fraction on the 4° grid: 0.332 (cell fraction; area-weighted truth is ~0.29 — equirectangular grids oversample the poles, equally for all models). Metrics: accuracy at P>0.5 and rank AUC. No gridded elevation ground truth was fetched; elevation claims are supported by band histograms, landmark spot checks (§5), and inter-model agreement.

5. Mechanism: the spatial index, not the facts

Small models possess the relevant textual facts (Qwen2.5 and Llama both answer 8848 to “How high is Mount Everest?”) but fail all six landmark probes at exact coordinates, including Everest, the Altiplano, and the Mariana Trench. Frontier models pass 6/6 (Astra, Opus; Fable 4/6 with one narration and one empty response).

Interpretation: name→fact retrieval is intact at all scales tested; coordinate→place indexing resolves ~103 km at 7–9B (adequate for continent blobs, useless for relief) and precisely at frontier. Land/water works where the index suffices; relief fails where it does not. Llama's refusal vs Qwen2.5's confabulation further dissociates uncertainty handling from knowledge possession.

6. Limitations

(1) Ground truth is 110m coastlines; sub-cell islands and exact shorelines are beyond it — some model "errors" are truth errors. (2) Equirectangular grids distort polar areas; all comparisons are grid-matched so this biases no model pair. (3) Frontier readouts are hard 0/1 (no logprob API), discarding uncertainty that local logprobs preserve. (4) Threshold cells mix peaks and valleys at 4°; cell-ambiguity is mitigated but not eliminated by exact-landmark probes. (5) Single seed and query order; no variance estimates. (6) Fable metrics carry a compliant-subset selection effect (narrated cells excluded). (7) Qwen3-8B excluded: its OpenAI-compatible endpoint returns no usable logprobs (thinking-block interaction) and native-API runs were land-biased mush.

7. Costs and compute

Local: ~150k queries, $0 (Apple Silicon, Ollama 4-bit quants). Frontier: ~60k queries, ~$200 OpenRouter spend across three models. Total wall time dominated by API throughput, roughly two days of background compute.
